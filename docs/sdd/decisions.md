# Product decisions

The original decision log distinguishes confirmed product agreements (DEC) from technical proposals (PROP). Preserve their identifiers, rationale, dates, and evidence. Append new maintained decisions in English and link the relevant historical agreements; do not silently renumber them. The historical log contains two DEC-025 headings; its second GitHub heading anchor is dec-025-1. This review preserves that ambiguity rather than assigning a new decision.

See the [maintained English guide](README.md) and the [complete Spanish historical record](../history/es/docs/sdd/decisions.md). This entry page is an English reading guide; the linked historical document retains the full original wording, criteria, and evidence.
