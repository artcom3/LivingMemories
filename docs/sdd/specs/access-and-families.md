# Access and families

Invitation-only adult access, family isolation, current membership checks, and narrator profiles without accounts are the core requirements. Protect the final administrator during concurrent membership changes.

See the [maintained English guide](../../access.md) and the [complete Spanish historical record](../../history/es/docs/sdd/specs/access-and-families.md). This entry page is an English reading guide; the linked historical document retains the full original wording, criteria, and evidence.
