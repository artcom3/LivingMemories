## Language and evidence

Use English for new documentation, headings, instructions, and decision updates. Keep source material, testimony, proper names, story titles, and quoted interface labels in their original language when needed. Translated content must remain linked to its originals.

The historical archive is a snapshot taken during this documentation review. It includes the working-tree content of the content-generation guide, including its existing English additions. It is not a record of a new deployment or a new test run. Do not update historical status claims to make them look current.
